Postdoctoral Researcher· University of California, Los Angeles

I study how ecosystems respond to and recover from disturbances. My focus are the tropical rainforests in western Ecuador, threatened by continuing deforestation, as well as forests in the western United States facing increasingly severe wildfires. I am also a passionate photographer and love to spend as much time outdoors as possible.
